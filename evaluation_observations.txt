The automated Bedrock evaluation completed successfully with a Builtin.Correctness score of 0.75 across four test cases.

The evaluation shows that the customer-support agent generally performs the required routing and response behavior correctly. The 0.75 correctness score indicates that there is still room for improvement. Overall, the evaluation demonstrates that the core routing, FAQ grounding, and bug-report workflow are functional, while highlighting specific response-format and handoff behaviors that could be refined in a future iteration.
